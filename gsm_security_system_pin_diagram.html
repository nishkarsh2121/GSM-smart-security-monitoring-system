⚠ Circuit image vs firmware discrepancy: The diagram labels HC-SR04 Trig as D9 (PB1), but the firmware configures PORTD |= (1<<PD2), which is D2. Wire the trigger to D2 as shown above. Also note the image incorrectly labels PD0 as "A0" — it is Digital 0 (RX). SIM800L VCC must use a separate 3.7–4.2 V LiPo supply, not Arduino's 5 V pin.
